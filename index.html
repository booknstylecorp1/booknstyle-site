FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still wondering about something? Email support@booknstyle.com or visit our support page.

How do I book an appointment?

Download BookNStyle, find a pro by category, name or city, then choose a service and an open time. You’ll get a confirmation and reminders in the app.

Is BookNStyle free to download?

Yes. BookNStyle is free to download, and you’ll see every fee before you pay.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. Every booking includes one free reschedule, up to your pro’s reschedule cutoff. You can also cancel from the app; each pro sets their own cancellation policy.

How do pros get paid?

Card payments pay out automatically to the pro’s bank account through Stripe. Cash payments are made in person and are 100% theirs. BookNStyle takes no commission on bookings.

I’m a pro. How do my clients find me?

Clients can discover you by category, name or city. You can also share your personal booking link or QR code with the clients you already have, so they can book you directly.

Who can use BookNStyle?

BookNStyle is for adults 18 and older in the United States.

How do I contact support?

Email support@booknstyle.com or visit legal.booknstyle.com/support. To delete your account and data, see Delete account.
